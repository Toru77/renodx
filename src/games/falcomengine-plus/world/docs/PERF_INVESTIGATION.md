# Performance investigation: perf_20261016-130551 and cache_diag_20261016-130617 (analysis only, nothing implemented)

Build `rt-panel-1`, RTAO on, Gather on, Collect cache diagnostics ON, GPU Debug off, Region 256, fade defaults. Files: `renodx-dev\falcomengine-plus\world\diag\`. Line refs are the tree at commit 82ad30ca. "Measured" = read from the files; "inferred" = arithmetic on the files plus code; "estimate" = not provable from the files.
Numbers below were computed from the `series` block (1804 presents: `frame_ms`, `cpu_total`, `tlas_rebuild`, `tlas_refit`).

## 0. Verdict

The cost is real and mostly the addon's own single-thread CPU work in the two present handlers, not RTAO and not the GPU. The game alone runs about 8 ms (the first 100 presents, TLAS about 5k instances: 10.7 ms with the addon). Frame time is the game plus about 8 to 9 ms of addon CPU that grows with the TLAS (rebuild 4.7 ms at 5k instances, 15.5 ms at 19k). Three things are wasteful, not necessary: (a) two full refits per present late in the run, (b) a TLAS that holds about 55 % of its instances beyond any distance RTAO can use, rebuilt in full every 8 presents for a 0.2 % change, (c) diagnostics and bookkeeping that scale with the pool and run in the hot path (Collect diagnostics after every rebuild, an orphan sweep every 10 presents, pool stats every present).

## 1. Where the frame time goes (question 1)

Fit over the 1804 presents: `frame_ms = 10.1 + 1.55 x tlas_rebuild + 0.94 x tlas_refit + 0.11 x other_cpu` (inferred, OLS). A measured ms of refit costs about one ms of frame; a measured ms of rebuild costs 1.55 ms (about 0.55 ms is unmeasured, section 5). The GPU rows do not enter (max `gpu_total` 6.9 ms, `gpu_rtao` max 6.1 ms, far below the 25 to 50 ms frames).

| Rank | Cost | Per-present average | Frame impact | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | TLAS rebuild | 1.48 ms measured, about 2.3 ms of frame | 92 % of the 164 hitches, every one of the 107 over 33.3 ms | exactly every 8 presents, 11.9 ms avg, 15.5 ms in the last 1100 presents |
| 2 | TLAS refit | 3.97 ms (24 % of frame), 5.9 to 6.6 ms in the late window | steady; all 13 non-rebuild hitches have refit 5.4 to 10.6 ms | 2292 refits in 1615 presents = two per present late |
| 3 | Hidden diagnostics at rebuild (inferred) | about 6.6 ms per rebuild present, about 0.8 ms average | rebuild present = 36 ms where rebuild 15.5 + refit change (-2.3) explains only 13.2 of the +19.8 | section 5 |
| 4 | Pool CPU: drain 0.92 (max 11.6), hooks 0.39, capture 0.08, deform 0.15 | about 1.6 ms | 10-present sweep adds 3.4 ms to one present in ten, 8 of the 13 non-rebuild hitches | section 6 |
| 5 | RTAO GPU 3.82 + filter 0.32 | 4.1 ms GPU | probably 0 to 1 ms of frame, concurrent with the CPU | section 7 |

Spike causes (series): of 164 frames over 25 ms, 151 are a rebuild present, 8 are the present with `i % 10 == 3` (the orphan sweep), 1 follows a 16 ms rebuild, 4 other. All 107 over 33.3 ms are rebuild presents. 224 rebuild presents are 12.4 % of presents but 22.6 % of wall time (6787 of 30013 ms); their excess over a normal present is 3494 ms = 11.6 % of the run. The present after a rebuild is normal (offsets +1..+7: 14.2 to 15.3 ms), so the rebuild does not leave a GPU backlog.
- The rebuild interval is exactly 8 presents in 215 of 223 gaps (`kLiveTlasInterval`, `bvh_live.hpp:75`; gate `:2165-2169`). It is a floor: `change` is never null (cache dump reasons: pool changed 375, store changed 131, visibility 10 of 519). The TLAS gauge was flat at about 4950 instances while it rebuilt every 8 presents (episode samples, offsets 97 to 1085), so even a stable scene rebuilds.
- Rebuild cost tracks instance count: 4.7 ms (presents 0 to 300), 8.5, 13.5, 16.3, 15.5 ms (1500+), about 0.83 us per TLAS instance.
- Frame time and rebuild: corr 0.85 (0.95 inside rebuild presents). Mean frame 30.3 ms in rebuild presents, 14.7 ms otherwise (36.6 vs 16.8 in the last 1100 presents).

## 2. Refit (question 2)

`RefitLiveTlas` (`bvh_live.hpp:1234`). The copy phase is only the dynamic suffix, not the full TLAS: `updated.assign(tlas_info.begin()+df, end)` (`:1267`), 3588 instances. It is under `g_pool.mutex` (`:1250-1289`), but the lock is not the cost: `pool_hooks` max is 1.0 ms while the lock is held for 14 ms in a rebuild.
- 2.60 ms per present = 2.05 ms per event / 3588 = 0.57 us per instance. Per instance, under the lock: a `lower_bound` in the 18.5k-instance pool (`:1271`, about 14 probes into a large struct array, cache-missing), five memcpy (`:1278-1281`), `GetPoolCameraVisibility` (MatrixHash plus a hash lookup in `g_pool.observations`, `bvh_pool.hpp:3088`, `:1282`), `ApplyPoolWindSway` (two more hash lookups, one `pow`, four `sin`, a 4x4 inverse, `:1283`, `:560-630`). The 192-byte descriptor is not the problem; the pool-side lookup and the wind maths are (breakdown inferred: no sub-lap exists inside phase 0).
- Phases 1 to 3 are full-TLAS work: copy all leaves and nodes (`:1316`, `:1346`, about 0.7 and 1.0 MB), `RefitBvhNodes` and `ValidateBvh` over all nodes (`:1347-1348`), upload both whole buffers (`:1360-1363`) although only the dynamic leaves changed. 0.70 + 0.30 + 0.20 ms per present; small next to the copy.
- Two refits per present. `UpdateLiveBvh` refits whenever `WindSwayPending` (`:504`, `:2178-2183`) and `RefitLiveTlasAfterDrain` refits again after the drain when `dynamic_revision` moved (`:2210-2214`). Wind inputs do not change between the two, so the second recomputes all 3588 sways. Measured: 2292 events over 1615 refitting presents = 677 extra refits x 3.1 ms = 1.17 ms/present; late-window per-present refit median 6.6 ms (p10 5.7, p99 8.4) is two refits of about 3.3 ms. The after-drain call is gated by a revision any follow bumps, even when P2b keeps that instance out of the TLAS (hypothesis, check `refits_after_drain` and `refit_after_drain_skipped` in `world_pool.json`).

## 3. Wind and dynamic instances (question 3)

3588 of 15245 (23.5 %) are dynamic: `copy.dynamic = instance.dynamic || copy.wind` (`bvh_live.hpp:932`), and wind is on by default. The split wind versus moving is not in the files (`tlas_wind_count` and `wind_pass.applied/zero_disp/clamped` are in `world_pool.json`, not supplied). The sway is a function of time (`pool_transform.hpp:141-151`: `gust = 1 - (0.5 sin + 0.5)^7`, which is near 1 most of the time), so with wind on nearly every wind instance changes pose every present. The memcmp at `bvh_live.hpp:1327` only skips the upload, not the maths.
- The movement is small: `cap = min(10 % of extent, 0.75 m)` (`:575-576`), so an instance under 1 m moves at most 10 cm, under 3 m at most 30 cm, while AO rays are 2 m and the result is accumulated over about 10 frames (history 0.9).
- Waste, by distance: RTAO weights AO to zero past the fade end, 80 m here (31.25 % of 256), and rays are 2 m. A wind instance farther than fade end + ray + sway bound (about 83 m) cannot change any pixel that is output. From the pool histogram about 55 % of in-region instances (at most 10.1k of 22.3k within 82 m Chebyshev) are beyond that. If wind instances follow the same split, a far-skip removes about half of phase 0: 0.7 to 1.7 ms/present (estimate; needs `wind_pass` data).
- Cadence option (every second present): about half of the sway cost, about 1.4 ms/present, visible only as 30 Hz sway steps in the AO. Owner decision (it changes output).

## 4. Rebuild spikes (question 4)

Phase split (cache dump, avg/max ms): snapshot+lock 6.71/14.08, filter+descriptors 1.80/3.76, sort+build+validate 1.49/3.12, buffer create 0.71/3.37, destroy 0.21/0.79. In the perf window phase 0 is 7.4 ms per rebuild (61 %).
- The snapshot copies every in-region instance (`bvh_live.hpp:907-933`, about 260 to 350 bytes each, 6 MB) although `added` is 17122 over 519 rebuilds (33 per rebuild, 0.2 % of 18k), `moved` 85. There is no `reserve` before `emplace_back` (`:913`), so the vector regrows about three times (about 13 MB of extra copying and fresh-page faults per rebuild), then `std::stable_partition` (`:935`) moves it again, then `info.push_back(instance)` copies it a third time (`:1005`). Per instance it also runs `GetPoolCameraVisibility` (`:929`, a hash lookup) and `ApplyPoolWindSway` (`:931`, two lookups even for non-wind instances).
- Minimum to avoid the spike: the work must be O(changes), not O(N): a change journal at the `BumpPoolRevision` sites (`bvh_pool.hpp:1937, 2294, 2917, 4671, 4773`), a persistent snapshot, in-place apply. The existing slack path (default off) cannot do it because it runs after the full snapshot (`:886-934` before `:1038`) and 219 of 249 of its attempts were "slack structural" (CPU_SPIKE_PLAN.md). `skip_noop` cannot either: only 33 of 519 rebuilds (6.3 %) are no-ops (cache dump `tlas.noop.static`). Do not spend more on those two switches.
- Cheaper without redesign: reserve and reuse scratch vectors, skip visibility lookups for unchanged instances (the pool keeps `visibility_revision`), a per-mesh wind flag instead of two lookups, a smaller TLAS (section 9, items 5 and 6). Moving the sort/build (phase 2, 14 %) to a worker saves little; phase 0 is the cost.

## 5. Unattributed CPU (question 5)

`unattributed = present_world + present_bvh - InPresent rows` (`perf_run.hpp:239-260`), 1.08 ms average: real work inside the two handlers that has no row. Names: `UpdateCacheDiag` (`bvh_live.hpp:2241`; tier pass over all pool instances every 30 presents under the pool lock, `cache_diag.hpp:130`), `RecordCacheTlasRebuild` (`bvh_live.hpp:1070`, called after the rebuild timer stops: two sorts of about 15k pointers in `DiffTlasInstances` `cache_diag.hpp:209-248`, `CacheEnteredIds` `:251`, and a `fresh_ids` set over all 26k pool instances while the episode is open, `:469-481`; the episode shows `open, t80 pending` the whole session), the two `LiveSnapshotMatchesTlas` no-op tests (`:1145-1154`, excluded from the rebuild time by design), `RunBvhDebugPass`, `RunP2bDiagnostics`, `DrainDeformProbe`, `MaybeCaptureRtaoStats`, `ResolveIndirectDraws`, the perf harness itself. Collect cache diagnostics defaults to ON (`cache_diag.hpp:403`).
- The 29.5 ms maximum is mostly an accounting artifact, not a 29 ms task. `bvh::Use` registers `OnWorldPresentBvh` before `OnWorldPresent` (`world.hpp:46` vs `:69`), the perf sample is read mid-present in `UpdateCacheDiag` (`bvh_debug.hpp:245`) and the bracket timers add on exit, so brackets lag the rows by one present and the per-present clamp (`perf_run.hpp:663-675`) turns the mismatch into positive spikes. Series proof: `cpu_total[i]` correlates 0.75 with `tlas_rebuild[i-1]` and 0.37 with `[i]`; the largest `cpu_total` values (38.8 at present 1752, 38.1, 36.3, 35.8) are all the present after a rebuild. The series mean 9.63 vs the file's 8.16 is the same clamp (file note in ROADMAP). Averages over the whole window are right; per-present unattributed and `cpu_total` are shifted by one present.
- The hidden part is real: a rebuild present costs about 6.6 ms more than rows explain (inferred; late window, 36.3 vs 16.5 ms, rebuild 15.5, refit 3.7 vs 6.0). Not proven to be the diagnostics (a GPU stall in buffer creation is the alternative); the A/B in section 10 separates them.

## 6. Pool CPU (rank 4)

- A bump of +3.4 ms mean (p90 6.0) on `i % 10 == 3` presents, none at other phases of 60 (series, non-rebuild presents, 158 samples). The only 10-present job in the drain is `RetirePoolOrphans(frame, PoolNewestSeenByKey())` when `frame % 10 == 0` (`bvh_pool.hpp:5802`): it builds an `unordered_map` over all 18 to 26k instances with a per-key pose vector (`:1864-1877`) under `g_pool.mutex`, then scans them again (`:1938`). Cost about 0.34 ms/present average, 8 of the 13 non-rebuild hitches. Inferred from period and code; no per-present drain series exists.
- `UpdatePoolStats` loops all pool instances, slots, meshes and keys every present under the lock (`bvh_pool.hpp:1970-2014`) for panel counters only: a few tenths of a ms (estimate).
- `FollowPoolMovingInstance` scans all pool instances per followed sighting (`bvh_pool.hpp:2884`, `mesh_id` compare over 18 to 26k instances): O(pool x moving draws), the likely source of the 11.6 ms drain maximum (hypothesis, cannot be proven here).

## 7. RTAO GPU (question 6)

`gpu_rtao` 3.82 ms avg (max 6.08), filter 0.32. It is one timer around pass A (trace) plus pass B (temporal) (PERF_COST_LIST.md #24), so the split is unknown. Pass A is one thread per pixel at full game depth size, 2 rays per pixel (`g_rtao_samples = 2`, `rtao_state.hpp:19`), ray length 2 m, radius 1 m, `world_rtao.cs_5_0.hlsl:149`. It is dominated by pixels x rays, not by the BVH: a TLAS of 22k instances is about 14 levels deep, a 40 % smaller TLAS saves under one level.
- Waste: the trace runs for every pixel before the fade is applied (`:149` then `:163-164`); a pixel past the fade end gets `ao = lerp(1, x, 0) = 1` exactly. An early-out when `distance_to_camera >= fade_end` is byte-identical in the AO output. Saving = pixels past 80 m (not in the files; `world_rtao.json` pixel stats would say).
- Knobs: Samples 2 to 1 (about half of pass A; noisier AO in motion, temporal weight 0.9 hides most), Spatial Filter off (0.32 ms, softer), Ray Max 2 to 1.5 m (shorter rays, slightly less contact range), half-resolution trace (not implemented; `ScaledResolution` is refused today). GPU is not the limiter: the first 100 presents run at 10.7 ms with all GPU work, and RTAO's maximum (6.1 ms) cannot create a 25 to 50 ms frame.

## 8. Lighting dark (question 7)

A label, not a cost. `dark` = no lighting pass ran for this frame's camera (`bvh_live.hpp:2273`): menus, cutscenes, loading (7 runs, longest 356 presents, 865 of 5629 presents). In the perf window `gpu_rtao` has 1742 samples in 1804 presents, so at most 62 presents (3.4 %) were dark; 92 % of the hitches are rebuild presents, so dark time does not drive them. The TLAS still rebuilds and refits in dark presents (waste only in menus, not worth a change).

## 9. Priority list (gain = estimate unless "measured"; none validated in game)

1. Region 96 to 128 with Fade Start/End raised to keep 80 m (config only, no code, no code risk). Region 128: end 62.5 % and start 31.25 %; Region 96: 83 % and 42 %. The TLAS box is `3 x Region + 20 m` (`CurrentPoolRegion`, `bvh_pool.hpp:1725-1742`), so 256 holds everything to 266..522 m. From the tier table about 22 % (Region 128) to 30 % (Region 96) fewer instances: rebuild -3.4 to -4.6 ms (-0.5 to -0.7 ms/present), refit phases 1 to 3 -20 to 30 %, fewer hitches over 33 ms. Output: same AO inside 80 m. Test: two 30 s runs, compare `tlas_instances`, `tlas_rebuild` avg per event, `frame_time`.
2. One refit per present: do the wind refit only in `RefitLiveTlasAfterDrain`, keep rebuild and fail branches in `UpdateLiveBvh` (`bvh_live.hpp:2176-2185`, `:2197-2226`). Gain -1.17 ms/present measured (events 2292 to 1615), about -3 ms late. TLAS at the end of each present is the same, so RTAO is identical; only the GPU Debug view sees poses one present older. Default ON. Test: harness fingerprint pin `0x55593b70e070e7fe`, a case comparing the TLAS bytes after a present both ways; in game `tlas_refit.events` about 1.0 per present.
3. Collect cache diagnostics gated by need (A/B first, section 10). Gain about -0.8 ms/present and -6 ms per rebuild present if the inference holds. Gate `RecordCacheTlasRebuild`'s diff/fresh-set, the no-op tests and `ComputeCacheTiers` to a dump request or visible panel, not every rebuild. Output identical. Changing the default to OFF is an owner decision (default stays ON until then).
4. Cheaper snapshot (`bvh_live.hpp:886-935, 1005`): reserve, reuse scratch, skip visibility and wind lookups for static instances. Gain -3 to -4 ms per rebuild (-0.4 ms/present). Byte-identical. Test: `TestRebuildPhases`, fingerprint, `rebuild_phase_0`.
5. Orphan sweep cost, not cadence (`bvh_pool.hpp:1864, 5802`): reserve the map, build poses only for keys with an old instance. Gain up to -0.3 ms/present and removes 8 hitches per 30 s. Byte-identical. A cadence change (every 30) delays ghost retirement by 20 frames and touches the P-3 ghost hold: owner decision, default off.
6. Adaptive rebuild interval, `interval = max(8, rebuild_ms_last / budget)` (`bvh_live.hpp:75, 2167`). With 15 ms and a 0.8 ms/present budget: 19 presents, 224 to about 95 rebuilds per 30 s, -0.8 ms/present, spike height unchanged. Output: new instances reach the TLAS up to 10 presents later (their median first-seen-to-TLAS is already 31 to 120 presents). Default OFF until the owner decides.
7. Wind far-skip, default OFF: in the refit skip the sway for wind instances beyond fade end + ray max + sway bound (`bvh_live.hpp:1268-1288`). Gain 0.7 to 1.7 ms/present. RTAO output identical in the AO range, the GPU Debug view differs, so a switch. Needs `wind_pass` data first.
8. RTAO early-out beyond fade end (`world_rtao.cs_5_0.hlsl:149`). Byte-identical AO; GPU gain unknown until the past-fade pixel share is measured; small frame gain while CPU-bound.
Estimated total of 1 to 5: about -3.5 ms average (16.6 to about 13 ms), rebuild present 36 to about 22 ms. The 8-present saw-tooth stays; removing it needs the change journal (section 4), a round of its own with a plan first.

## 10. What the files cannot prove, and the data that settles it

- Hidden rebuild cost (section 5): A/B two 30 s runs, Collect cache diagnostics ON vs OFF, same route and map settled. Expect the `series` rebuild-present frame to drop by about 6 ms if the diagnostics are the cause.
- Per-present GPU series: `series` has no GPU rows, so GPU spikes cannot be excluded; only `gpu_rtao` max 6.1 ms is known. Needs `gpu_rtao` and `gpu_total` in `series`, or a GPU Debug ON run (it also adds a trace). RTAO's true frame cost needs one run with RTAO off (BVH on) and one with the addon off.
- Wind vs moving split and how many wind instances change pose: `world_pool.json` (`tlas_wind_count`, `wind_pass.applied/zero_disp/clamped/samples`, `refits_after_drain`, `refit_after_drain_skipped`, `refit_instances`).
- Drain spikes (max 11.6 ms), `deform_update_cpu` max 7.7, `mesh_capture` max 5.2: no per-present series, so their correlation with the hitches is untested.
- Phase 0 sub-split (lock wait, copy, partition, lookups): add laps.
- The run was a refill: the TLAS grew from about 5k to 19k during the window (episode samples), so the late 5.5 to 6 ms refit and 15.5 ms rebuild are the settled-map numbers only if the map stayed filled. Repeat on a settled map.
- Screen resolution and the share of pixels past 80 m (RTAO early-out value).
